About the company

Known facts, simply stated.

Habbah Innovations Limited is based in Kampala, Uganda. This profile uses the available public directory record while leaving unconfirmed company history and operations unstated.

Habbah sits at the meeting point of enterprise, food and agriculture in its current public profile.

The confirmed company contact is Mwesi Y Habagaya. A public business directory classifies the company under accommodation and food-service activities. A separate value-chain listing associates Habbah with rice and identifies processing, milling and packaging.

These entries offer a useful starting point, but not a complete operational story. The site therefore avoids claiming a restaurant, hotel, factory, packaged brand, production capacity, partnership network or certification.

Businesses and prospective collaborators are invited to contact Habbah directly for current and authoritative information.

Profile principle
