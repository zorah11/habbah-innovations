About the company

Known facts, simply stated.

Habbah Innovations Limited is a Kampala-based enterprise with a focus on food, agriculture and the rice value chain.

Habbah sits at the meeting point of enterprise, food and agriculture.

Based in Kampala, Habbah Innovations Limited is represented by Mwesi Y Habagaya. Its business focus connects food and agriculture through the rice value chain.

The company’s areas of interest include rice processing, milling and packaging—three stages that help move grain towards the market.

Businesses and prospective collaborators are welcome to get in touch to explore Habbah’s current offering and opportunities.
